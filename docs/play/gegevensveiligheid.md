# Play Console – Data safety form (DRAFT answers)

**Does the app collect or share data?** Yes (collect); not shared with third parties.
**Encrypted in transit?** Yes (HTTPS).
**Can users request deletion?** Yes (clear app data / contact) – no account.

| Category (Play) | Type | Collected | Shared | Optional | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Location | Precise location | Yes | No | No (core of the app) | App functionality, fraud prevention (fake reports) |
| App activity | Other user-generated content (reports) | Yes | No* | Yes | App functionality |
| App activity | In-app search history (destinations) | No** | – | – | – |
| Device or other IDs | Device ID (random) | Yes | No | No | Fraud prevention (one vote per device) |

\* Confirmed reports are publicly visible without personal data; Play does not count that as "sharing".
\*\* Searches are only forwarded in order to search and are not stored (recent destinations stay on the device only).

**Ephemeral processing:** the location for the online counter and route requests are kept in
memory only ("ephemeral").
